# Restride privacy policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026._

Restride is a training app for people who lift and run. It uses a morning check-in and your Apple Health data to adjust today's session. This page says what it does with your information. The short version: **your information stays on your iPhone, and we never receive it.**

## What Restride stores

- **What you tell it:** your check-ins, plan, workouts and sets, injuries and limits from your physio, places you train, notes and your name if you give one.
- **What it reads from Apple Health, with your permission:** sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, workouts, steps and body weight. It uses these to decide how hard today's session should be and to show the runs you recorded with other apps. You can turn any of them off in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Restride. Restride then works from your check-in alone.
- **What it writes:** Restride does not write to Apple Health. If you have an Apple Watch, it can send your planned runs to the Workout app on the Watch through Apple's WorkoutKit. You choose whether to allow this.
- **Notifications:** if you turn on the morning reminder, Restride schedules it on your iPhone. No server is involved.

## Where it goes

Nowhere. All of this is stored on your iPhone.

- Restride has **no servers, no accounts and no sign-in**.
- It has **no analytics, no advertising, no tracking** and no third-party code that collects data.
- It does **not sell or share** your information with anyone, because it never has it.
- It does **not use iCloud** to sync its own data between your devices.

Two things outside Restride's control are worth knowing:

- **Apple Health** keeps your health data under your own Health settings, which may include syncing through iCloud. That is between you and Apple.
- **Device backups.** If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Apple includes app data in the backup, as it does for every app.

## Health information and medical advice

Restride is not a medical device and **isn't medical advice**. It never diagnoses or treats anything. When you tell it something hurts, it changes the session to train around it and suggests seeing a physio or doctor for sharp or lasting pain. Please talk to a health professional about any health concern before you change how you train.

## Your choices and control

- Allow or stop Restride's Health access any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
- Turn off the reminder and the Watch connection in Restride's You tab or in iOS Settings.
- **Delete everything** by deleting Restride from your iPhone. Because nothing is sent anywhere, nothing is left on our side.

## Children

Restride isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

## Changes

If this ever changes, for example if Restride adds sync or an account, this page will be updated first and the app will ask you before anything leaves your iPhone.
